🗺️ Journey Map & GPS Stops

At a station or attraction, tap Mark current location. JourneyMate saves your GPS coordinates and the current date and time. Location permission and a GPS signal are required.

Place names are looked up automatically from GPS coordinates. Enter a name only if the lookup fails, or rename a saved stop.

GPS works only after granting location permission. Position accuracy may be reduced inside trains or station buildings. Automatic place-name lookup and map tiles require internet, but saved coordinates remain available offline. This feature records individual stops, not continuous background tracking.
